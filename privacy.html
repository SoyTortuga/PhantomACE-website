The gist: we keep what Twitch hands us at sign-in and whatever you choose to post — that's it. No advertising, no analytics watching you, nothing about you sold to anyone, and it all lives on our own server instead of scattered across a dozen clouds.

01
What we collect

From Twitch, when you sign in. Signing in happens on Twitch's own pages — we never receive or store your Twitch password. We ask for two read-only permissions, user:read:follows and user:read:subscriptions, so we can tell whether you follow and subscribe to the channel, and we store:

DataWhy we have it
Your Twitch user idThe stable key for your account. It's how we know you're still you when your username changes.
Your username and display nameShown everywhere you appear on the site.
Your avatar URLShown beside your name. The image itself is served by Twitch, not copied here.
Your follow & subscription statusRead at sign-in to set your role (follower, subscriber tier). We do not ask for or store your email address.

From you, as you use the site. Profile content (banner, chosen badge and title, showcased cosmetics, room), forum threads and replies, profile comments, chat messages, and game activity — results, stats, streaks, leaderboard positions, inventory, and your dinos and progress — plus settings like your notification preferences.

From PhantomACE's stream systems. Watch time (Phamily Time), subscriber tier, VIP status and moderator status, channel-point activity and hype-train participation. These come from Twitch and the stream bot; the site reads them to apply your roles and rewards.

Technical data. Standard server logs, including IP address, produced by the web server and by Cloudflare, which sits in front of the site.

02
What we do with it
  • Run your account, show your profile, and keep you signed in.
  • Run the games, leaderboards, forums, chat and rewards.
  • Apply the roles and perks that depend on your follower, subscriber, VIP or moderator status.
  • Moderate the site and enforce the guidelines, including acting on reports.
  • Diagnose faults from server error logs.

We don't build advertising profiles, we don't do behavioural tracking, and we don't sell or rent personal data. There is no data broker on the other end of any of this.

03
What is public

Your profile page, username, avatar, badges and titles, roles, game stats, leaderboard entries, forum posts and profile comments are public — visible to anyone, signed in or not. Profile pages can be indexed by search engines, so if you'd rather your PhantomACE profile didn't turn up in a search for your username, that's the thing to know before you fill it in.

Your notification settings and any moderator notes about your account are not public.

05
Where it lives, and for how long

Site data is held in our own database on the server behind phantomace.tv, along with uploaded images such as badge art. It isn't spread across third-party cloud storage.

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Posts, comments and chat messages are kept while they're on the site. A deleted message keeps its row but is blanked out, so a conversation doesn't collapse into nonsense around the gap.
  • Sign-in sessions expire on their own and are cleared.
  • Moderation records are kept after the content is gone — a record of why an account was actioned is the only thing that makes an appeal answerable.
06
Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account through the Discord. That removes your profile, your inventory and cosmetics, your game progress, and blanks your posts and comments on this site.

What deletion here does not reach: your watch time, subscriber history and channel status live with Twitch and PhantomACE's stream systems, not with this site — this site only mirrors them. Deleting your PhantomACE account doesn't delete anything there, and if you sign in again with the same Twitch account, those figures flow back onto a fresh profile. As long as you keep watching on Twitch while signed in to Twitch, watch time keeps being counted. This is how viewer statistics work across the whole streaming ecosystem, and it isn't something this site controls.

07
Your choices and rights

Most of your profile is editable in-app. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, reach us on the Discord, naming the Twitch account in question, so we can be sure it's you.

08

You must be at least 13, matching Twitch's minimum age. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13; if we learn we have, we remove it.
