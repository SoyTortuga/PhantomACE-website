What is stored

  • pham_session — one signed cookie holding your Twitch id, name, avatar and role. It is what keeps you signed in, and it expires after 24 hours.
  • In this browser — your theme, the notifications above, and per-game things like a guest name or whether a game's sound is muted. Clearing site data resets those and nothing else.
  • On the server — your profile, inventory, saves and scores, keyed to your Twitch id. Logging out does not delete them; ask in the Discord if you want them gone.
