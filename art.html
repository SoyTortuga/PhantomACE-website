Here's where the images on phantomace.tv come from: pixel art made for the games, asset packs licensed from other artists, artwork created for the site's badges and branding, and a few pieces of third-party content that a fan game needs to be the game it is. This page says which is which.

01
Where the images come from
WhatWhere it comes from
Game art, sprites and tilesetsThe games are built in-house; much of their pixel art is hand-made or hand-assembled, and some is built on licensed asset packs (see below).
Licensed asset packsSome game art is bought from working artists under a commercial licence — for example creature and tileset packs sourced from itch.io — resized and recombined to fit the games.
Badges, banners and key artCreated for the site.
Logos and brand marksThe PhantomACE identity and logo.
FontsUnifrakturMaguntia and Cinzel Decorative (open-source, via Google Fonts), plus the display faces used for titles and headers.
02
Third-party content we don't own

Commander Bingo and MTGBBB are fan games played along with the stream. The Magic: The Gathering card images, set symbols and related marks they use are owned by Wizards of the Coast and its affiliates. We didn't make them, we claim no ownership of them, and we're not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Wizards of the Coast.

Twitch emotes shown in some games are PhantomACE's own channel emotes, served by Twitch. If a rights holder wants something removed, reach us on the Discord and we'll take it down rather than argue about it.

03
Member uploads

Content posted by members — clips, images, profile art — belongs to them, and the statements above cover the art the site itself ships, not member uploads. Members are responsible for having the rights to what they post (see the Terms).

04
Corrections

If you think something here is credited wrong, or want to know where a specific image came from, reach us on the Discord with the page and the image and we'll tell you. If we get something wrong we'll fix it here rather than quietly swap the file. This page is updated when the answer changes; the date at the top is the last review.
