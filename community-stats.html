How subscribing changes your rate

Subscribe and your boost starts from that moment. Anything you had already banked keeps its old rate, and every hour from your sub onward counts at your tier's rate.

If your sub ends, nothing you earned is taken back. You carry on from right where you are, now earning at the base rate again.
