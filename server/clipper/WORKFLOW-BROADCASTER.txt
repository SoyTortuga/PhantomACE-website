===============================================================
 CLIP BOT - WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
===============================================================

Short version: about 5 minutes of your time, twice, and then
you're done forever.

The bot takes your best Twitch clips, turns them into vertical
videos, and posts them to YouTube for you. I handle all of it.
The only thing I can't do for you is approve access to your own
channels - that has to be you.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT IT WILL POST
---------------------------------------------------------------

  Magic: The Gathering clips  ->  PhantomACEMTG
  All other game clips        ->  PhantomACEtoo
  IRL / Just Chatting / Music ->  never posted, ever

Two videos per channel per day, picked from your best clips of
the last year. Anything new that's getting views gets bumped to
the front of the line.

Videos are scheduled a week ahead, so there's always a queue
sitting ready. You can see it in YouTube Studio under Content.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT I NEED FROM YOU
---------------------------------------------------------------

1. Make sure both channels exist:
      PhantomACEMTG
      PhantomACEtoo

2. Approve access to each one. I'll send you a link per channel.
   Steps are below.

That's everything. I never need your password.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 THE LINK - WHAT TO EXPECT
---------------------------------------------------------------

*** Do it while you're sat at a computer. The link stops
*** working after a few minutes. If it expires just tell me and
*** I'll send a fresh one - no harm done.

1. Open the link I send you.

2. Sign in to the Google account that owns the channel I named.
   If you've got several Google accounts, open the link in an
   incognito/private window to be safe.

3. You'll see a warning: "Google hasn't verified this app."
   This is expected. It says that because it's a tool I built
   for you, not a public app.
   Click "Advanced", then "Go to PhantomACE Clipper".

4. Click Allow. The only thing it can do is upload videos to
   that one channel. It can't read your email, it can't delete
   anything, it can't touch your other accounts.

5. Your browser will then show an ERROR PAGE - something like
   "can't reach this site", with 127.0.0.1 in the address bar.

   THIS IS SUPPOSED TO HAPPEN. You haven't broken anything.

6. Copy the WHOLE address out of the address bar and send it
   to me. That's the bit I need.

Then we do it once more for the other channel.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 AFTER THAT
---------------------------------------------------------------

Nothing. It runs on its own.

Videos will appear in YouTube Studio marked Scheduled, with a
date about a week out. They go live on their own at 10am and
2pm Pacific.

If you ever want to kill a video before it goes live, just
delete it in YouTube Studio. The bot won't put it back.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 TURNING IT OFF
---------------------------------------------------------------

You're in control of this, not me.

To cut off access completely, any time, without asking anyone:
   myaccount.google.com
   -> Data & privacy
   -> Third-party apps & services
   -> PhantomACE Clipper -> Remove access

It stops posting immediately. Tell me if you do that so I'm not
hunting for a fault that isn't there.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 TIKTOK (later)
---------------------------------------------------------------

Same idea, but TikTok makes apps go through a review first.
Until that's approved, anything the bot makes shows up as a
PRIVATE DRAFT in your TikTok app rather than going live - so
you get the final say on every one.

YouTube isn't waiting on this. It can start straight away.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 THINGS WORTH KNOWING
---------------------------------------------------------------

- I never see or need your password.
- The access is only ever "upload videos to this channel".
- Everything stays yours - the channels, the videos, the access.
  If I ever stop working on this, you keep all of it and I lose
  access, not the other way round.
- Some clips have game music in them. YouTube occasionally mutes
  or blocks those. Nothing either of us can do about it - just
  delete the video if it happens.
