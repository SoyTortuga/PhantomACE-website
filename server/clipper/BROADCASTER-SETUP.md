# PhantomACE Shorts Bot — What I need from you

Hi! I'm setting up an automated tool that takes your best Twitch clips and
turns them into vertical videos (YouTube Shorts + TikTok), sorting them by game
so each game can post to the right account. I'll handle all the technical work.
A few things can **only be done by you**, because they involve your own accounts
and your brand. Here's your part — nothing here needs any coding.

Expect this to take about **1–2 hours of your time total**, most of it in one
screen-share session with me.

## 1. Give the go-ahead
Confirm you're happy for your clips to be re-posted to YouTube and TikTok, and
tell me:
- Which **games** should be promoted, and which **account** each game's clips
  should post to (you can use one account for everything, or a separate YouTube
  channel per game — your call).
- Roughly how often and how many per day (my default is 2 per account per day).

## 2. Make sure the destination accounts exist
- The **YouTube channel(s)** you want the Shorts to land on. If you want a
  separate channel per game, create those channels (I can walk you through it).
- The **TikTok account** you want to post to.

These stay fully yours. I never need your passwords.

## 3. Sit with me for one setup session (~30–45 min)
We'll do these together on a screen share. You'll do the logging-in and
clicking "Allow"; I'll handle everything around it:
- **Connect each YouTube channel** — you sign in and approve access so the tool
  can upload to that channel.
- **Connect the TikTok account** — same idea: you sign in and approve.
- **Accept the YouTube and TikTok terms** when prompted (you're the account
  holder, so it has to be you).

## 4. TikTok's approval step (I prepare, you submit)
TikTok requires an app to pass a short **review** before it's allowed to post
videos publicly. Until that's approved, TikTok posts will arrive as **private
drafts** that you finish posting from the TikTok app yourself.
- I'll prepare the whole submission.
- You'll review it and submit it under your brand.
- Meanwhile, **YouTube can go fully live right away** — we don't have to wait on
  TikTok.

## What you keep control of
- You can **disconnect the tool at any time** from your Google and TikTok
  account settings — no involvement from me needed.
- All the accounts, channels, and the videos are **yours**. If we ever stop
  working together, you keep everything and I lose access, not the other way
  around.
- I never store your passwords. The tool only gets a revocable "permission
  slip" to post, which lives on your streaming rig.

## What you don't need to worry about
I handle all of this: building and running the tool, the video editing/branding,
choosing the clips, the server setup, and keeping it running.

That's it — once we've done the setup session, it runs on its own.
