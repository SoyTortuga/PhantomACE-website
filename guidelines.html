Community Guidelines

How we treat each other here, and what happens when someone doesn't

Last updated 23 September 2026 Version 1.0 Applies to phantomace.tv

This is the PhantomACE community hub — a place to hang out between streams, play games with people you recognise from chat, and be part of the Phamily. These guidelines exist so that stays fun for everyone. They apply everywhere on the site: the forums, profile comments, chat, game lobbies, giveaways, and your inventory and profile.

01
The short version

Treat people the way you would if PhantomACE were reading over your shoulder, because a moderator probably is. If you wouldn't say it in his chat, don't post it here.

02
What is not allowed
  • Harassment and targeted abuse. Going after a specific person, pile-ons, following someone thread to thread, or using profile comments to needle somebody.
  • Hate. Slurs or attacks based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation. There is no "it was a joke" exemption.
  • Sexual content. Keep it off the site. No nudity, no explicit writing, no suggestive avatars or banners. Anything sexual involving minors is reported to law enforcement and ends the account permanently.
  • Threats and violence. Including the ones you think are obviously jokes.
  • Doxxing. Don't post anyone's real name, address, workplace, school, phone number or private messages — including your own family's details.
  • Spam and self-promotion. Link dumps, referral links, repeated posts, and "check out my channel" everywhere. Sharing your own stuff in the right thread, when it's actually relevant, is fine.
  • Scams. Fake giveaways, account trading, impersonating PhantomACE, a moderator or staff, or asking anyone for their login details. Nobody here will ever ask you for your Twitch password.
  • Cheating. Scripts, bots, alternate accounts, exploiting a bug for rewards, or teaming up to rig a leaderboard. If you find a bug that pays out, report it and we'll thank you rather than ban you.
  • Illegal content, or anything that breaks Twitch's Terms of Service. Your account here comes from Twitch, so their rules ride along with it.
03
Things that are fine

Swearing, trash talk in games, arguing about a bad take, memes, being loud. This is not a quiet place and it's not supposed to be. The line is people, not language: aim it at an idea and you're fine, aim it at a person and you're not.

04
Your profile

Your bio, banner, equipped badge and title, and showcased cosmetics are public, and they're held to the same standard as a post. A profile is not a loophole.

05
Reporting

Forum posts, profile comments and chat messages can be reported, and a report goes to the moderation queue, not to the person you reported. You won't be told the outcome, because telling you would tell you what happened to someone else's account.

Reporting somebody because you disagree with them, or mass-reporting to bury a post, is itself a guideline break.

06
What moderators can do

Moderation is deliberately granular, so one bad week doesn't have to cost you the whole site. Roughly in order of severity:

ActionWhat it means
Content removedA post, comment or chat message is taken down. Forum posts keep an edit history, so a removal is recorded rather than silent.
Media removedA clip or image you submitted to the gallery is taken down.
Forum restrictionsYou may lose the ability to start threads, or to reply at all, while keeping the rest of your account.
Chat / comment restrictionsYou can't post in site chat, or can't comment on profiles. Existing content stays.
Game removalRemoval from one specific game without touching the rest of your account.
Account lockedAccess to the site is removed. Because your account is your Twitch account, being banned from PhantomACE's Twitch channel carries here too.
07
Appeals

If you think a moderator got it wrong, reach us at contact@phantomace.tv or through the Discord with your Twitch username, what was removed, and why you think it was a mistake. Arguing about it in public, or making a new account to get around a restriction, turns a temporary problem into a permanent one.

08
These guidelines change

The site gains games and features regularly, and the rules follow. The version and date at the top of this page tell you which revision you're reading. Substantial changes are announced on the site.
