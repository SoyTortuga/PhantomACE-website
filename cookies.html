Cookies

What the site puts on your device, and where we stand on advertising

Community Guidelines Terms of Service Privacy Notice Art & Assets Cookies
Last updated 23 September 2026 Version 1.0 Applies to phantomace.tv

phantomace.tv sets one cookie, and it exists purely to keep you signed in. There is no advertising cookie, no analytics cookie, and no tracking pixel on this site. That's why you've never seen a consent wall here.

01
The cookie we set
NameWhat it doesLife
pham_session Your sign-in session. It holds a signed token with your Twitch id, display name, avatar and role, so the site can show you as signed in and render your name and badge without an extra request. It's cryptographically signed on our server, so editing it in a browser just logs you out rather than changing anything. Marked SameSite=Lax so it isn't sent from other sites, and Secure so it only travels over HTTPS. Expires on its own after 24 hours, and is cleared when you sign out.

This is a strictly necessary cookie. Without it there's no way to stay signed in, so there's nothing meaningful to consent to and nothing to switch off other than not signing in.

02
Things stored in your browser that aren't cookies

Some things are kept in your browser's own storage instead. They never leave your device and aren't sent to us:

  • A guest id. So you can play the games without signing in, and keep your place in a game room. It stays on your device.
  • Local game saves and settings. Things like a Skull Clicker save, sound and volume choices, and small per-game preferences.

Clearing your browser's site data for phantomace.tv removes all of it, and the site carries on working with defaults.

03
Cookies other people set

Parts of the site load content from other companies, and they set their own cookies under their own policies. We can't switch those off from here.

WhoWhen
TwitchSigning in, and the embedded stream player on the home page.
CloudflareEvery page, since it sits in front of the site. It may set a security cookie to tell real visitors from attack traffic.
Google FontsEvery page, to load two of the site's typefaces. No cookie, but Google's servers see your IP address.
04
Advertising

There are no ads on phantomace.tv, and no ad tech behind the scenes — no ad network, no advertising identifier, no retargeting pixel, and no data sold or shared for cross-context behavioural advertising. If that ever changes we'll say so on this page before it ships, and give you the controls the law where you live requires. We won't quietly switch on tracking and update this page afterwards.

05
Analytics

We don't run a web-analytics product. There's no script on the page counting your visits or following you between pages. What we do have is ordinary server logs, which is how faults get found.

06
Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Since the site does no advertising and no cross-site tracking, there's nothing here for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to turn off. We honour them by not doing the thing in the first place. If we ever add anything they apply to, we'll respect the signal.

07
Managing cookies yourself

Every browser lets you view and delete cookies and block third-party ones. Blocking our session cookie will stop you signing in. Blocking Twitch's may break the embedded player. Everything else on the site will still work.

08
Changes

This page is updated whenever the site adds or removes something that touches your device. The date at the top is the last review. Questions go to contact@phantomace.tv.
